# Insurance Data Analysis

## Objective:
Conducted a comprehensive data analysis of an insurance company's claims, policies, and customer demographics using SQL, Power BI, and Excel. The goal was to derive actionable insights to enhance business decision-making, optimize claim processing, and improve customer segmentation strategies.

## Key Features:
## Policy and Customer Analysis:
- Identified the total number of policies (10,004K) and analyzed the distribution of active vs. inactive policies, revealing 58.13% active policies.
- Examined the gender-wise distribution of policyholders, with a near-equal split between male (5003) and female (5001) customers.

## Claim Insights:
- Investigated claims by status, showing 4.4K rejected, 3.4K settled, and 2.3K pending claims.
- Broke down claim amounts across different policy types, with health and auto insurance contributing the most to total claims.
- Analyzed claim status by age group, revealing that adults (8.8M claims) had the highest number of claims, followed by elders (6.4M) and young adults (1.7M).

## Revenue and Premium Analysis:
- Evaluated premium collection by policy type, with travel insurance generating the highest premium revenue (2.5M), followed by health (1.2M) and auto (1.0M).
- Identified high-value policyholders and regions with maximum claim settlements, aiding in risk assessment and policy pricing optimization.

## Regional and Policy Type Trends:
- Assessed pending, rejected, and settled claims across different policy types, helping improve policy underwriting decisions.
- Discovered growth opportunities in underperforming policy segments, leading to targeted marketing strategies.
  
## Results:
- Identified key policy segments driving revenue and regions with high claim settlements.
- Provided actionable insights for optimizing policy pricing, improving claim processing efficiency, and enhancing customer retention strategies.
- Helped the company refine marketing strategies by identifying top-performing policy types and customer demographics.
Skills:
Power BI, data visualization, Excel, business analytics, claims processing analysis, and dashboard design.
